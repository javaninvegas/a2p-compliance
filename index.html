SMS Privacy Policy

Enlight Your Mind LLC — Last updated: March 2026

Text Messaging Program

Enlight Your Mind LLC provides home health care nursing services. We may send care-related text messages including check-ins, reminders, and support messages to patients who have opted in to receive them.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How We Collect Consent

During an in-person nursing visit, your nurse will ask if you would like to receive text messages for care check-ins and support. If you verbally agree, we will send you one confirmation SMS asking you to reply YES to confirm your opt-in. You are not subscribed to receive ongoing messages until you reply YES. Consent is not a condition of any service.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.

To opt back in, reply START.

For help, reply HELP or contact us at (725) 888-8755.

Types of Messages

  • Care check-in messages from your assigned nurse
  • Appointment reminders and scheduling updates
  • Wellness and support messages

Messages are care-related only. We do not send marketing, promotional, or sales messages.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number or any personal information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your contact information is used solely for the purpose of delivering care-related communications from your assigned nurse.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. Your phone number and healthcare information are stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel involved in your care.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your text message enrollment, please speak with your nurse or call us at (725) 888-8755.
