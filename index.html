SMS Privacy Policy

Giving Home Health Care — Last updated: March 2026

Text Messaging Program

Giving Home Health Care may send appointment reminders, medication check-ins, and wellness messages to patients who have provided written consent. You will only receive messages if you have signed a written consent form at intake.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

How We Collect Your Phone Number

Your mobile phone number is collected at the time of your initial home healthcare intake appointment. You provide written consent by signing our patient intake form, which includes a specific authorization for text message communications. No messages are sent until written consent is obtained and on file.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.

To opt back in, reply START.

For help, reply HELP.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number or any personal information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your contact information is used solely for the purpose of delivering healthcare-related communications from your assigned nurse.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. Your phone number and healthcare information are stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel involved in your care.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your text message enrollment, please speak with your nurse or call our office directly.
