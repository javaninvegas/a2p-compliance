Patient SMS Consent Information

Enlight Your Mind LLC

How Opt-In Works

Step 1: During an in-person nursing visit, your nurse asks if you would like to receive text messages for care check-ins and support.

Step 2: If you verbally agree, we send you one confirmation SMS referencing your prior agreement and asking you to reply YES to confirm.

Step 3: Only after you reply YES are you subscribed to receive ongoing care-related messages.

Consent is not a condition of any service.

What Messages You May Receive

  • Care check-in messages from your assigned nurse
  • Appointment reminders and scheduling updates
  • Wellness and support messages

Messages are care-related only. No marketing, promotional, or sales messages are sent.

Important Information

  • Message frequency varies
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out
  • Reply HELP for assistance
  • Your phone number will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes
